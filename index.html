Allineamento n-ario

L’allineamento dispone di un target collettivo con più unità testuali; non richiede corrispondenze 1:1.

dsc:Alignment

Classe

An addressable observation that two or more textual units participate in one correspondence.

Sottoclasse di
oa:Annotation, prov:Entity
Note d’uso
An alignment is n-ary, symmetric at the level of correspondence and non-transitive. Translation direction remains on the expression-level dsc:translates relation.
Elemento TEI
link inside linkGrp[@type='translation']
